## 1. Internship Overview
This summer internship requirement was completed through the **GenAI Powered Data Analytics Job Simulation** provided by Tata iQ on Forage. The simulation places the learner in the role of an **AI Transformation Consultant at Tata iQ**, working on a simulated financial-services problem for Geldium Finance.

The main objective is to analyze customer data, predict credit-card delinquency risks, and develop recommendations for intervention strategies while considering ethical, explainable and responsible AI. The official simulation is self-paced, intermediate, and designed for approximately **3–4 hours**.

**Official program:** https://www.theforage.com/simulations/tata/data-analytics-t3zr

## 2. Internship Objectives
- Understand data-driven decision-making in financial services.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) and risk profiling.
- Identify missing, inconsistent and problematic data.
- Understand potential indicators of credit-card delinquency.
- Explore predictive modeling and model-selection concepts.
- Understand model validation and evaluation.
- Use Generative AI as an analytical assistant.
- Convert analytical findings into business recommendations.
- Understand fairness, transparency, explainability and responsible AI.
- Develop analytical reporting and business communication skills.
